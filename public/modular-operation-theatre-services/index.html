System Specifications

Component System Detailed Engineering Matrix
Wall & Ceiling Matrix 0.8mm - 1.0mm thick EGP/PPGI modular panels with anti-bacterial polyurethane coating. Seamless 50mm radius aluminum coving eliminates 90-degree corners.
Laminar Air Flow (LAF) SS 304 plenum with terminal HEPA filters (99.997% efficiency at 0.3μm). Creates unidirectional, non-turbulent sterile air flow at 90±20 FPM.
Hermetic Doors Microprocessor-controlled sliding doors that drop and seal inward. Optional 1mm-2mm Pb equivalent lead lining for radiological shielding.
Conductive Flooring 2mm homogeneous, seam-welded vinyl on copper grounding grid to safely discharge static electricity and protect micro-electronics.
Surgeon Control Panel Flush-mounted, IP65-rated digital interface consolidating HVAC controls, MGPS alarms, timers, and peripheral lighting circuits.
Illumination Dynamics Aerodynamic cleanroom LED fixtures ensuring >500 lux ambient light without disrupting the critical downward laminar airflow pattern.

Frequently Asked Questions

Expert insights on Modular Operation Theatre setup and compliance.

Compliance requires a rigid criteria of air changes per hour (ACH), precise temperature and humidity control, HEPA filtration (0.3 microns at 99.997%), seamless antibacterial surfaces, and positive pressure gradients to prevent cross-contamination. Medgenz engineers ensure all these parameters are meticulously met, documented, and certified before final handover.
Depending on the site readiness and specific customizations, a standard Modular OT setup takes between 4 to 6 weeks from the approval of the 3D CAD design to the final HEPA DOP validation and handover. Our prefabricated modular approach guarantees speed without compromising engineering quality.
Yes, Medgenz operates a dedicated pan-India logistics and engineering deployment network. We handle the entire lifecycle—from manufacturing at our centralized HQ to on-site assembly, final testing, and comprehensive annual maintenance contracts (AMC) across any state in India.
Absolutely. Our EGP (Electro-Galvanized steel) and PPGI panels are backed with fire-retardant Gypsum or high-density PUF core insulation. This offers excellent thermal insulation, acoustic dampening, and rigorous fire safety compliance suitable for high-risk hospital environments.
Terminal HEPA filters typically last between 12 to 18 months, heavily dependent on the operational load and the quality of primary and secondary pre-filters in the AHU. Medgenz recommends and performs bi-annual DOP (Dispersed Oil Particulate) testing to precisely monitor filter integrity and plan replacements proactively.
